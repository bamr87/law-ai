Title 18 - Criminal Code

Article 3 - Offenses Against the Person

Effective: July 1, 2024

§ 18-3-101. Criminal homicide defined - classification.

Effective: July 1, 2024

(1) A person commits criminal homicide if the person causes the death of another person.

(2) Criminal homicide is murder in the first degree, murder in the second degree, manslaughter, criminally negligent homicide, vehicular homicide, or murder of a peace officer or firefighter.

§ 18-3-102. Murder in the first degree.

Effective: July 1, 2024

(1) A person commits the crime of murder in the first degree if:

(a) After deliberation and with the intent to cause the death of a person other than himself or herself, he or she causes the death of that person or of another person;

(b) Acting either alone or with one or more persons, he or she commits or attempts to commit arson, robbery, burglary, kidnapping, sexual assault, escape, or class 3 felony child abuse...

§ 18-3-103. Murder in the second degree.

(1) A person commits the crime of murder in the second degree if the person knowingly causes the death of a person.

§ 18-3-104. Manslaughter.

(1) A person commits the crime of manslaughter if such person recklessly causes the death of another person; or such person intentionally causes or aids another person to commit suicide.

§ 18-3-105. Criminally negligent homicide.

Any person who causes the death of another person by conduct amounting to criminal negligence commits criminally negligent homicide which is a class 5 felony.
