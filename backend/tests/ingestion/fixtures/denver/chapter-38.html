Chapter 38 - LAW ENFORCEMENT, FIRE PROTECTION AND OTHER PUBLIC SAFETY

Sec. 38-31. Definitions.

As used in this article, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates a different meaning.

Peace officer means any person certified by the state as a peace officer and employed by the department of safety.

(Code 1950, § 811.1; Ord. No. 342-05, § 1, 5-31-05; Ord. No. 20-11, § 2, 1-18-11)

Sec. 38-32. Interference with a peace officer.

It shall be unlawful for any person to knowingly obstruct, impair or hinder the enforcement of the law or the preservation of the peace by a peace officer acting under color of the officer's official authority.

(Ord. No. 501-08, § 3, 9-15-2008)

Sec. 38-33. Reserved.

Reserved.

Sec. 38-34.5. Unlawful discharge of a firearm.

It shall be unlawful for any person to knowingly or recklessly discharge a firearm within the city, except at a range licensed under article IV of this chapter or in the lawful defense of person or property.

(Ord. No. 88-14, § 1, 2-24-14)
